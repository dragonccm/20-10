Bloom identity

Original sprout mark: two soft leaves and a stem, on a violet rounded square.
Violet #8b5cf6, pale leaf #e9dcff, wordmark #344054.

Assets:
- bloom-mark.svg / .png: square symbol, 64px vector / 512px raster.
- bloom-wordmark.svg: outlined wordmark for in-app navigation.
- bloom-logo.svg / .png: combined mark and wordmark, transparent background.
- bloom-logo-light.svg: white lettering for dark backgrounds.

The wordmark uses outlined Plus Jakarta Sans at weight 650, matching the Studio
font. It requires no external font request. Font license and provenance:
app/studio/fonts/OFL.txt and SOURCE.txt in the project repository.

app/icon.svg uses the same mark. app/favicon.ico includes 16, 32 and 48px sizes.
app/apple-icon.png is the 180px Apple touch icon.
